# Knot & Bloom: Vision & Strategy

This document serves as our North Star. It outlines the core goal, vision, and the specific problems Knot & Bloom exists to solve. Whenever we make product, design, or business decisions, they should be measured against these principles.

---

## Our Goal

To become the most **trusted and curated** marketplace for handcrafted goods in the Philippines. We connect independent artisans directly with buyers by removing the noise of mass-produced drop-shipping and solving the trust deficit of social media commerce.

## Our Vision

We envision a digital ecosystem where local craftsmanship is protected and celebrated. By providing strict curation and a highly secure infrastructure, we empower small creators to scale their businesses sustainably, while giving buyers absolute confidence that they are purchasing authentic, ethically made goods from verified local makers.

---

## The Problems We Are Solving

### 1. The Curation Problem (The "Sea of Mass Production")
*   **For Buyers:** Finding genuine handcrafted items on Shopee or Lazada is nearly impossible; they are buried under thousands of cheap, factory-made imports.
*   **Our Solution:** Strict product and seller curation. Every artisan and item is vetted, ensuring that when a buyer comes to Knot & Bloom, 100% of the catalog is authentic and handcrafted. We are a sanctuary from mass production.

### 2. The Trust Deficit in Social Commerce
*   **For Buyers:** Buying from Instagram or Facebook requires a massive leap of faith—sending money to a stranger's bank account and hoping the item ships. Scams are rampant.
*   **Our Solution:** We act as the trusted intermediary. We handle secure payments, offer order tracking, and manage dispute resolutions, making buying from a local student hobbyist as safe as buying from a major retailer.

### 3. The Financial Risk of COD (Cash on Delivery)
*   **For Sellers:** COD is the lifeblood of Philippine e-commerce, but "bogus buyers" (people who refuse the package) force small artisans to absorb the cost of materials and return shipping—a fatal blow to micro-businesses.
*   **Our Solution:** Our **20% COD Deposit** and **Customer Trust Score** system. We weed out bad actors before the item is even made, ensuring sellers only spend their time and materials on committed buyers.

### 4. The Operational Burden for Artisans
*   **For Sellers:** Managing DMs, manually checking GCash receipts, and organizing shipping takes time away from their actual craft.
*   **Our Solution:** We provide an automated, trusted infrastructure—from AI-assisted product listings to unified order tracking—so they can stop being full-time customer service reps and focus on being artisans.
