# How Shipping Works on Knot & Bloom

We know shipping in the Philippines can feel unpredictable — especially outside Metro Manila, where the nearest courier branch might be a whole town away. So instead of guessing, here's exactly how we calculate shipping on Knot & Bloom, and why.

---

## Two Ways to Get Your Order

### 🤝 Pickup — Free
Meet your maker (or their designated pickup point) in person. No shipping fee, no waiting for a courier.

- Available when the buyer and seller are close enough to meet conveniently — usually the same or a neighboring town.
- For safety, sellers share a **public meet-up point** (like a plaza, terminal, or barangay hall) rather than a home address.
- Great for last-minute gifts or when you just want to support a local maker face-to-face.

### 🛵 Delivery — Fee Shown Before You Checkout
Your item comes to you, either hand-delivered by the seller or sent through a courier/cargo service.

- The shipping fee is calculated automatically based on where the seller and buyer are located — you'll always see it before you pay, never as a surprise at checkout.
- Depending on distance, your order may be delivered personally by the seller (common for nearby towns), or shipped through a bus cargo counter or courier partner (for farther destinations).

---

## Why Does the Fee Vary So Much?

Because the Philippines isn't one-size-fits-all when it comes to shipping. A courier branch might be right around the corner in the city — but in a lot of the towns our makers come from, the nearest one is an hour away. So we don't charge a flat "national" shipping rate. Instead, we look at **how the item can realistically travel** between the two locations:

| If you and the seller are... | Delivery usually looks like... | Typical Fee |
|---|---|---|
| In the same town | A quick local trip | ₱30–50 |
| In neighboring towns *(e.g. Cantilan ↔ Madrid)* | The seller drives it over themselves | ₱70–110 |
| Farther apart, same province | Bus or van cargo counter | ₱120–180+ |
| Different provinces/regions | A courier partner (LBC, J&T, etc.) | ₱150–250+ |
| Different islands | Sea or air cargo | ₱250–450+ |
| Very remote / island barangay | Arranged directly with the seller | Varies |

*(All fees are estimates and shown exactly at checkout before you pay — nothing here is final pricing.)*

---

## When a Seller Delivers It Themselves

A lot of our makers don't have access to a courier branch nearby, so many deliveries happen the way small towns have always done it — the seller hops on their own motorcycle or tricycle and brings it to you personally. When that's the case, the fee isn't arbitrary. It's built from two honest costs:

1. **Fuel for the round trip** — the seller has to get there *and* back home.
2. **A small amount for their time** — packing, prepping, and personally delivering your order takes time away from their craft.

We use current local fuel prices to keep this fair and update them periodically — so if pump prices go up, the fee reflects that, and if they drop, so does the fee. This way, sellers aren't quietly losing money on gas just to get your order to you, and you're not overpaying for a trip that's actually just down the road.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is Pickup free but Delivery isn't?**
Because with Pickup, you're doing the traveling — there's no fuel or courier cost for the seller to cover. With Delivery, someone (the seller or a courier) has to physically move your order to you, and that has a real cost.

**Can I request Pickup even if I'm from another town?**
Pickup is only offered when it's realistic for both sides — usually within the same or a neighboring town. If you're farther away, you'll see Delivery options instead.

**What if the seller doesn't have a vehicle?**
No problem — in that case, your order is sent through a hired local rider or a bus/van cargo counter instead, and the fee reflects that option.

**Why did my delivery fee change slightly from a previous order?**
Fuel prices in the Philippines shift frequently. We periodically update our delivery pricing to reflect current, real costs rather than locking in an outdated rate.

**What if I live somewhere with no courier coverage at all?**
Some very remote barangays genuinely have no scheduled delivery route. In that case, you'll see a note to coordinate shipping directly with the seller before placing your order.

**Is my delivery fee refundable if I cancel?**
Shipping fees follow the same cancellation and refund rules as the rest of your order — check your Order Timeline for the current status, or reach out to support if you're unsure.

**What if my item arrives late because of bad weather?**
The Philippines gets a lot of typhoons, and provincial routes are affected more than city ones. If a delivery is delayed due to weather, this will be reflected on your Order Timeline as a weather delay, not a cancelled or lost order.
